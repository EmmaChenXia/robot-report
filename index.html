资料 01 · 南华早报

机器人企业挣扎于商业化变现

ROBOT MAKERS GRAPPLE WITH MONETISATION · 2026年6月5日

▶ 一句话结论

资本持续涌入，但中国人形机器人企业普遍面临"能造不能盈"的困境，商业模式尚未跑通。

137笔
2026年中国机器人行业融资笔数，披露金额超 285亿元
73.6%
宇树科技收入来自研究/教育客户，真实商业化客户不足三成
30%

国家电网采购机器人金额，工业检测场景，属真实需求信号
关键事实
  • 宇树科技营收17.1亿元，净利润仅6亿元；产品均价76万元
  • UBTech目标部署1万台，争取两年内盈亏平衡
  • 智元机器人采用"数据收集工厂"模式，通过租赁平台Sharebot扩展商业模式
  • 国家电网68亿元采购是少见的真实工业端需求验证
  • 官员已明确警告行业存在泡沫风险
来源：South China Morning Post（2026/06/05）
Lead
Capital continues to flood into China's robotics sector, but profitability remains elusive as challenges emerge from industry's business model.
资本持续涌入中国机器人行业，但随着商业模式挑战浮现，盈利依然难以实现。
Scene
A desk with a laptop and a stack of books. A shirt waiting to be folded. A kitchen counter, a milk-tea stand and a pile of building blocks, all in a single room. Robots of different shapes face their tasks with focus, completing each with the help of a human operator.
一张摆着笔记本电脑和一叠书的桌子，一件等待折叠的衬衫，一个厨房台面、一个奶茶台和一堆积木，全都在同一个房间里。各种形态的机器人专注地面对各自任务，在人类操作员协助下逐一完成。
Factory Model
Pick up. Place. Fold. Repeat. This is not a film set, it is a data collection factory for embodied intelligence, a de facto "data foundry". The facility in Shanghai's Zhangjiang hi-tech zone is run by Agibot, a fast-rising robotics company.
拿起，放置，折叠，重复。这不是电影片场，而是一座具身智能数据采集工厂，实际上就是“数据铸造厂”。该设施位于上海张江高科技园区，由快速崛起的智元机器人运营。
Data As Product
The data, which is scarce and a key bottleneck, is not only used to train the firm's in-house models, but is also for sale, at prices that can reach several hundred yuan per hour. This non-mainstream monetisation offers a glimpse into how robotics companies are scrambling to diversify revenue.
这些数据既稀缺又是关键瓶颈，不仅用于训练公司自研模型，也对外销售，价格每小时可达数百元。这种“非主流”变现方式，折射出机器人公司正如何加速寻找多元收入来源。
Core Tension
From hardware sales and data services to leasing and enterprise solutions, companies are testing multiple paths. "The technology is no longer the biggest uncertainty, the business model is," said Zhong Sheng, head of China industrials research at Morgan Stanley.
从硬件销售、数据服务到租赁和企业解决方案，企业都在尝试。摩根士丹利中国工业研究主管钟晟指出：“技术已不再是最大不确定性，商业模式才是。”
2026 Capital Wave
He added that 2026 would be a critical year as humanoid integrators strive to reach commercialisation and build ecosystems. Capital continues to flood into the sector, with multiple startups announcing major fundraising rounds and some valuations reaching tens of billions of dollars.
他补充称，2026年将是人形机器人集成商冲刺商业化与生态构建的关键年。资本仍在持续涌入，多家初创企业宣布大额融资，部分估值已达数百亿美元。
Financing Snapshot
According to Qichacha, the sector has seen 137 financing deals across 112 related brands in 2026 so far, with disclosed funding exceeding 28.5 billion yuan. A highlight is Unitree Robotics' listing progress, seeking to raise about 4.2 billion yuan.
据企查查，2026年至今行业已发生137笔融资，涉及112个品牌，披露融资额超过285亿元。焦点之一是宇树科技上市推进，拟募资约42亿元。
Commercialization Stage
While monetisation paths were broadening, most remained in verification and trial stages, even as China's humanoid deliveries were expected to double to 28,000 units this year.
尽管商业化路径在拓宽，但多数仍处于“验证与试验”阶段，即便中国人形机器人今年交付量预计将翻倍至2.8万台。
Deployment vs Profitability
Companies have pushed deployments in EV, battery and electronics factories and logistics parks, but heavy R&D spending and the experimental nature of orders continue to weigh on profitability.
企业正推动在新能源车、电池、电子工厂与物流园区的部署，但高研发投入与订单试验属性仍持续压制盈利能力。
UBTech Metrics
UBTech's full-size robots were priced at an average of 760,000 yuan per unit, reaching about 30 per cent of a human worker's productivity in box-handling tasks in 2025, with this year's target at 50 per cent. The company aims to deploy 10,000 robots this year and break even within two years.
优必选全尺寸机器人均价约76万元，2025年在搬箱任务中效率约为人工30%，今年目标为50%。公司计划今年部署1万台，并在两年内实现盈亏平衡。
Scale Loop
Real-world deployments drive both revenue and data, reinforcing a loop between scale, model improvement and use-case expansion. "Scaling production and deployments remain the core challenge," said chief brand officer Michael Tam.
真实部署同时带来收入与数据，强化“规模-模型改进-场景扩展”的正向循环。首席品牌官Michael Tam表示，“扩大生产和部署规模仍是核心挑战”。
State Grid Demand Signal
State Grid Corporation of China was reportedly set to spend 6.8 billion yuan on robot procurement this year for inspection and high-risk tasks, from makers including Unitree, UBTech, Deep Robotics, Agibot and Fourier.
据报道，国家电网今年将投入68亿元采购机器人用于巡检和高危任务，供应商包括宇树、优必选、深之蓝、智元和傅利叶等。
Agibot Expansion
Shanghai-based Agibot, founded in 2023 by former Huawei engineers, has taken a broader approach spanning multiple products, enterprise pilots, leasing, and a data collection and trading platform called Maniformer, plus supply-chain investments and ODM business.
总部位于上海的智元机器人由前华为工程师于2023年创立，采取更广泛路线，覆盖多产品、企业试点、租赁、Maniformer数据采集与交易平台、供应链投资及ODM业务。
Revenue and Factory Workflow
Founder and chairman Deng Taihua said the firm generated 1 billion yuan in revenue in 2025. At the data collection factory, operators repeat assigned routines for hours, generating four to five hours of usable data per day, with each completed action logged as a data point.
创始人兼董事长邓太华表示，公司2025年营收达10亿元。在数据采集工厂，操作员按任务长时间重复动作，每人每日产生4至5小时可用数据，每个动作都被记录为数据点。
Data Output and Clients
According to staff during a recent SCMP visit, the factory can produce up to 30,000 entries daily. Clients are usually enterprises seeking task-specific datasets.
据SCMP近期到访时现场人员介绍，该工厂每日最多可产出3万条数据。客户通常是需要特定任务数据集的企业。
Sharebot Leasing Network
"For now, the most effective way to drive circulation is through leasing," said Li Kewei, chief marketing officer of Agibot's rental platform Sharebot. The platform connects partners nationwide, including those with order access and secondary development capability, with robot supply from Agibot and other brands.
智元租赁平台Sharebot首席营销官李可威表示：“目前推动机器人流通最有效的方式是租赁。”该平台连接全国合作伙伴（包括能接单与二次开发者）与智元及其他品牌机器人的供给。
Scale First
Partners rent and deploy machines to meet local demand. The business reportedly completed fundraising in the "hundreds of millions" of yuan by end-April. Li said immediate goals were scale rather than profitability, with this year's targets at 10,000 monthly rental orders and 1,500 service providers.
合作伙伴通过租赁并在本地部署机器满足需求。该业务据称在4月底完成“数亿元”融资。李可威称，短期目标是规模而非盈利，今年目标为每月1万单租赁与1500家服务商。
Home Scenario and Competition
While most major domestic robot makers are cautious on household applications due to complexity, X Square said it planned to bring a new robot into homes as early as this month, partnering with 58.com to assist cleaners with household tasks. As Tesla pushes mass production of Optimus this year, competition among integrators is expected to intensify.
由于复杂性，多数国内头部厂商对家用场景仍较谨慎；但X Square表示最早本月将推进机器人入户，并与58同城合作，协助家政清洁任务。随着特斯拉今年推动Optimus量产，集成商竞争预计将进一步加剧。
Forward Look
Morgan Stanley said in a recent note that development of the robot "brain" and reliability of dexterous hands would be key focus areas in industrial and service settings. Additional reporting by Minxiao Chang.
摩根士丹利近期指出，底层模型能力（"大脑"）与灵巧手可靠性将成为工业与服务场景的关键关注点。（Minxiao Chang参与报道）
⭐ 核心结论

商业模式尚未跑通，多数企业靠教育/研究客户维持。真正的工业/消费端部署仍处早期。资本热度与实际盈利能力之间存在显著落差，泡沫风险真实存在。

“千亿级新赛道”最近被央视财经用在移动充电机器人上。导轨充电桩与移动充电机器人等柔性补能模式，已在上海等地落地应用。

这不是概念，也不是PPT，而是已经在停车场真实运行。2025年产业规模达570亿元，预计2030年突破3200亿元，年复合增长率超过40%。

A commuter in Shanghai used to struggle with occupied chargers and blocked parking spaces. Now she books via phone, and a robot moves to her car and starts charging.
上海通勤用户过去常遇到“桩被占、位被堵”的问题。现在手机预约后，机器人会自动移动到车辆旁完成充电。

该模式对场地依赖更低：在老旧小区可替代固定桩不足，在高速服务区高峰期可机动补能。湖南已在春运期间部署移动充电机器人。

首个充电机器人国家标准已启动编制，行业开始进入“立规矩”的规范化阶段。

对新能源车主而言，正在从“人找桩”转向“电来找人”，补能服务上门的体验正变成现实。
资料 03 · Bloomberg Primer

人形机器人：炒作与现实的差距

Humanoid Robots and the Gap Between Hype and Reality · 24分钟

▶ 一句话结论

Physical AI方向正确，但"机器人数据缺口"和执行器灵巧度不足是两大核心技术瓶颈，商业化时间表被严重高估。

⚠️ 被明确点名的技术瓶颈
  • 灵巧度（Dexterity）：抓取、力控、手指精细操作是最难复制的人类能力
  • 机器人数据缺口：物理动作数据几乎从未被系统记录
  • 电池续航短：限制实际工作时长
  • 速度慢：仓储测试中效率远低于人工
🔧 数据采集三种方式及局限
  • 仿真训练：规模大，但缺乏真实物理细节
  • 现实视频挖掘：难以提取三维信息
  • 遥操作：真实但极慢，有专属"数据农场"
1X最新方案：世界模型（World Model）
预测动作 → 执行 → 学习，形成飞轮效应
🌏 中国的位置
✅ 优势
  • 2025年出货量全球第一
  • 140家企业，完整供应链
  • 与EV路径相同的垂直整合能力
⚠️ 风险
  • 出货量领先 ≠ 技术领先
  • 官员已警告泡沫风险
  • Physical AI核心技术仍有差距
💬 真实部署反馈 —— 仓储公司GXO实测
"机器人会损坏货物、放错位置、速度慢、电池续航短。需要人工全程监督纠错。未来几年不会取代工人，ROI尚不明确。"
来源：Bloomberg Primer（24分钟视频口播整理）
[00:00 - 00:22]
This hand, this knee, and this head are all small feats of engineering, decades in the making. And together, they are about to take a massive leap forward. Humanoid robots have exploded out of the lab and into the real world, not always on the best footing.
这只手、这个膝盖和这个头部，都是几十年工程积累下来的小奇迹。它们正在共同迈出巨大一步。人形机器人近来已从实验室走向现实世界，只是还不总是站得那么稳。
[00:20 - 00:53]
This is not just funny robot videos. Behind dancing and gimmicks is a very serious, very expensive and still unproven bet: AI-powered robots could one day transform labor as we know it. Interest in humanoid automation has existed for a long time, but commercialization is now being treated as viable.
这不只是搞笑机器人视频。舞蹈和噱头背后，是一场严肃、昂贵且尚未验证的赌注：AI机器人可能改变我们熟悉的劳动形态。人们对人形自动化的兴趣由来已久，但现在开始被当作可商业化的方向。
[00:50 - 01:43]
AI plays a large part in this push. The core hope is to crack physical AI: putting specialized intelligence into a body with sensors, batteries, and expensive actuators. It could become a multi-trillion-dollar market by 2050, but that will depend on real work in homes, hospitals, and especially factories.
AI在这一波推进中作用巨大。关键是攻克“物理AI”：把专用智能装进带有传感器、电池和昂贵执行器的身体。到2050年它可能形成数万亿美元市场，但前提是能在家庭、医院，尤其是工厂里真正干活。
[01:41 - 02:44]
There is plenty of hype from influential tech figures, but hype does not build supply chains or rewrite labor markets. In a 2025 demonstration, 1X's Neo still required remote human assistance, showing progress but also the remaining gap.
行业里有大量来自科技名人的炒作，但炒作并不能建立供应链或改写劳动力市场。2025年的一次演示中，1X 的 Neo 仍需远程人工协助，说明有进展，但差距依然存在。
[02:42 - 03:52]
Neo is built by 1X. The company says the robot's behavior is driven by AI end to end: images, forces and tactile inputs go into a large neural network, and motor commands come out, down to torque and force, for balancing, walking and manipulation.
Neo由1X打造。该公司表示其行为是端到端AI驱动：图像、力反馈和触觉等输入进入大模型，输出直接是电机控制命令，精确到力和扭矩，用于平衡、行走和操作。
[03:49 - 04:35]
Unlike traditional robots programmed for one fixed task, AI-based robots should learn over time in uncertain environments. This is part of physical AI, which also includes autonomous cars. The claimed advantage is that everything becomes data, not hand-coded rules for each task.
不同于只执行固定任务的传统机器人，AI机器人理论上能在不确定环境中持续学习。这属于“物理AI”范畴，也包括自动驾驶。其优势在于把任务转化为数据驱动，而不是每个任务单独硬编码。
[04:33 - 05:26]
1X is one of many startups entering the humanoid race. The recent frenzy is tied to AI investment booms, with billions invested in 2025 and soaring valuations worldwide.
1X只是众多人形机器人公司之一。近期热潮与AI投资爆发直接相关，2025年已有数十亿美元投入，全球估值快速抬升。
[05:24 - 06:53]
Big names like Nvidia, OpenAI, Google and Tesla are all betting on bringing AI into the physical world. Nvidia benefits from both chips and software stacks used to train generative and robotics models. Musk's projections for Optimus are especially aggressive, which further amplifies hype.
英伟达、OpenAI、谷歌、特斯拉等巨头都在押注“AI落地到物理世界”。英伟达在训练生成式模型和机器人模型的芯片与软件栈上都占优势。马斯克对Optimus的预测尤为激进，进一步放大了市场情绪。
[06:50 - 08:06]
Experts describe the current stage as progress mixed with clear incompleteness. Berkeley's Ken Goldberg frames the central question as the "GPT moment for robotics", but notes that LLM success in language does not directly solve robot action in physical space.
专家认为现阶段是“有进展但未就绪”。伯克利教授Ken Goldberg提出“机器人的GPT时刻”这一问题，但强调语言模型的成功并不能直接解决机器人在物理空间中的动作控制。
[08:03 - 09:52]
Chat models predict words; robots must generate motion commands from visual streams. That requires huge task data: grasping cloth, finger articulation, force control. Most physical experience has never been recorded, creating a major robot data gap.
聊天模型预测文字，而机器人需要从视觉流中生成动作指令。这要求海量任务数据，如抓布、手指关节控制、力控等。大量物理经验从未被系统记录，形成巨大的“机器人数据缺口”。
[09:49 - 11:03]
To close this gap, companies are building data libraries via simulation and real-world video mining. Simulation scales well but lacks some real-world detail; 2D video is abundant but weak for recovering full 3D structure.
为弥补缺口，企业通过仿真与现实视频挖掘构建数据库。仿真可规模化但缺真实细节；二维视频很多，但难以恢复完整三维结构。
[11:01 - 12:22]
Teleoperation provides high-quality real data but is slow and expensive. It is controversial in demos yet useful for training. Another path is flywheel learning: robots collect data while working, retrain models, and iteratively improve.
遥操作能提供高质量真实数据，但速度慢、成本高。它在演示中有争议，却在训练上有效。另一条路径是飞轮学习：机器人边工作边采集数据，反复训练并持续迭代。
[12:21 - 13:54]
1X says it increasingly uses a world model: the model predicts an action video, then compares with real execution to learn and improve, similar to a feedback flywheel.
1X表示其正更多采用世界模型：先预测动作画面，再与真实执行对照学习和修正，形成类似反馈飞轮的机制。
[13:52 - 15:30]
China is shown as moving fast in real-world rollout, including humanoid competitions and broad policy backing. Robotics has been treated as strategic priority alongside EVs and semiconductors.
报道显示中国在真实落地推进上速度很快，包括人形机器人赛事和政策支持。机器人与新能源车、半导体一样被纳入战略优先方向。
[15:28 - 16:44]
In 2025 China pledged major funding for emerging technologies, with robotics a key area. The country hosts around 140 humanoid firms, though officials have also warned of bubble risk amid rapid expansion.
2025年中国为新兴技术提供了大规模资金支持，机器人是重点之一。中国已有约140家人形机器人企业，但在快速扩张下，官方也提示了泡沫风险。
[16:22 - 17:20]
China's manufacturing scale and vertically integrated supply chain, from sensors and AI chips to batteries, helps with lower cost and faster deployment, similar to the EV playbook.
中国在传感器、AI芯片到电池的垂直一体化与制造规模优势，有助于降本和快速部署，这一路径与新能源车产业相似。
[17:18 - 18:46]
Still, for investment to pay off, robots must do more than backflips. Social impact depends on how workers are included in deployment. Humanoids are intended for multi-purpose tasks that traditional industrial robots cannot cover.
但要让投资真正兑现，机器人不能只会“炫技”。社会影响取决于部署过程中如何纳入劳动者。人形机器人的价值在于覆盖传统工业机器人难以胜任的多用途任务。
[18:43 - 19:40]
Large firms such as BMW, Amazon and Hyundai are running controlled pilots. These pilots identify where humanoids work or fail, while producing the operational data needed for future model improvement.
宝马、亚马逊、现代等大型企业正在开展受控试点。这些试点用于识别人形机器人的适用与失效场景，同时产出后续模型改进所需的运营数据。
[19:37 - 20:33]
Warehouse operator GXO says humanoids can help in specific environments, for example freezer operations where long human exposure is uncomfortable.
仓储企业GXO表示，人形机器人在特定环境下有价值，例如冷库作业，可减少人员长时间处于低温环境。
[20:30 - 21:41]
But current limitations remain clear: dropped or misplaced products, slow speed, limited battery life, and insufficient dexterity. Human supervision and correction are still required, and near-term ROI is uncertain.
但当前局限同样明显：掉货、错放、速度慢、续航短、灵巧度不足。现场仍需人工监督和纠偏，短期ROI尚不确定。
[21:39 - 22:40]
Factories and warehouses are easier early deployments because processes are structured. Homes and hospitals are harder due to uncertainty and far higher consequences of mistakes.
工厂和仓储由于流程更结构化，成为更容易起步的场景。家庭和医院不确定性更高，错误代价也更高，因此更难落地。
[22:38 - 23:40]
The closing view is cautiously optimistic: the field has long-term potential, but timelines must stay realistic. The key challenge is turning demonstrations into dependable, economically justified work.
结论是谨慎乐观：赛道长期潜力仍在，但时间预期必须现实。关键挑战是把“可演示”变成“可靠且经济可行”的真实工作能力。
⭐ 核心结论

技术路径清晰，长期潜力巨大（2050年万亿美元市场），但2-3年内不会大规模替代人工。灵巧度和数据缺口是当前最核心的技术瓶颈，也是最值得突破的方向。

🎯 三篇内容综合判断

大方向成立，但短期预期需要降温

三篇内容合在一起，描绘了同一个行业的三个切面：资本端热度极高、技术端瓶颈明确、落地端分化显著。人形机器人大爆发的条件尚不具备，但特定赛道（专用服务机器人）已经在高速增长。

📌 三个核心判断
① 行业长期有前景，但不是"马上爆发"

Bloomberg给出2050年万亿美元预测，充电机器人给出2030年3200亿细分赛道数据——时间跨度均在5年以上。SCMP所描述的整机厂商现状（效率30%、靠教育客户维生）表明，大爆发的条件尚不具备。三篇内容中没有任何一篇预测人形机器人会在2-3年内大规模替代人工。

② 泡沫风险真实，但结构性机会同样存在

SCMP明确引用官员对泡沫的警告；Bloomberg指出马斯克等人的预测"过于激进"。但充电机器人已跑出真实增长，国家电网68亿元的采购说明工业端需求是真实的。行业正在分化：整机层面泡沫风险高，特定应用场景和关键零部件层面机会更为扎实。

③ 中国有结构性优势，但"出货量 ≠ 技术领先"

三篇内容均肯定中国在规模和供应链上的领先，但Bloomberg和SCMP同时暗示：在Physical AI、灵巧度等核心技术上，中国企业与全球顶尖水平仍有差距。规模优势能支撑短期竞争，但技术深度决定长期位置。

🔩 对关节 / 灵巧手 / 机械臂业务的启示
⚠️ 以下观点严格基于三篇内容，不作额外推断
🦾
灵巧度是核心瓶颈
Bloomberg明确点名：抓取、力控、手指灵巧度是整机落地的直接阻碍。整机卡住的地方，正是零部件供应商的机会窗口。
🧠
端到端控制对硬件提出新要求
Physical AI（世界模型、端到端控制）要求执行器不只是"能动"，还需可感知、可学习——对关节设计和控制研究有直接参考价值。
📈
专用赛道增长确定性更高
充电机器人年复合增长>40%，已有国家标准背书。专用机械臂/执行器在这一赛道的近期商业机会，比人形机器人更为明确。
